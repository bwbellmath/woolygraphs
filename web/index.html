Measured on the same edges the optimizer works on: each stitch to its right-hand neighbour in the round, and each stitch to the one above it in its column. Column edges touching a decrease or a cast-on are counted as shaping, since they have no reason to sit at plain column gauge.
For each round above the equator the ideal slice width is round(2πR sinθ · st/in / slices) with θ = π/2 − rows/(rnd/in · R); the outlined cells are the two ends of that width, centred in the repeat. Radius defaults to brim circumference / 2π.
